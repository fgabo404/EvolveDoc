## Acquisition methods

These describe material handling, not the future number of UI actions or implemented recipes.

| Tag | Method | Source outcome | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `butchering` | Butchering | `requires_dead_animal` | Remove flesh, bones and raw fat from a usable carcass; not an automatic reward for sighting or killing. |
| `skinning` | Skinning | `requires_dead_animal` | Remove an intact skin; tanning is a separate process. |
| `recover_hard_parts` | Recover hard parts | `requires_dead_animal` | Recover present horns, tusks or baleen; do not assume every individual has them. |
| `recover_tendons` | Recover tendons | `requires_dead_animal` | Separate useful tendons; cleaning and preparation for cordage remain later processes. |
| `collect_shed_antlers` | Collect shed antlers | `collects_discarded_material` | Gather naturally shed antlers already present; does not remove them from a living animal. |
| `pluck_carcass` | Recover carcass feathers | `requires_dead_animal` | Recover feathers after death. |
| `collect_shed_feathers` | Collect shed feathers | `collects_discarded_material` | Gather feathers already shed; no live plucking. |
| `collect_eggs` | Collect eggs | `removes_egg_without_killing_adult` | Requires eggs already present. Removing a fertilized egg can reduce recruitment; reproduction rules remain pending. |
| `shear_and_release` | Shear and release | `preserves_animal` | Requires controlled restraint and a suitable coat; live capture does not imply domestication. |
| `comb_fiber` | Comb underwool | `preserves_animal` | Collect loose underwool with appropriate handling; no instant coat regrowth. |
| `collect_shed_fiber` | Collect shed fiber | `collects_discarded_material` | Gather already shed fiber; material must be present. |
| `recover_carcass_fiber` | Recover carcass fiber | `requires_dead_animal` | Recover suitable hair/underwool from a dead animal; do not also count the same coat as intact fur on a hide. |
| `milking` | Milking | `preserves_animal` | Only suitable managed lactating females; wild presence does not grant dairy production. |
| `collect_manure` | Collect manure | `collects_discarded_material` | Collect accessible droppings at managed husbandry locations. |
| `filleting` | Fish processing | `requires_dead_animal` | Separate fish flesh, present bony skeleton, skin and fat-bearing tissues. |
| `shellfish_processing` | Shellfish processing | `consumes_animal` | Opening/processing consumes the animal; pearls require an actual pearl-bearing individual. |
| `collect_empty_shells` | Collect empty shells | `collects_discarded_material` | Collect abandoned empty shells; yields no meat or pearl by itself. |
